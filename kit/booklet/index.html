Power and sleep

Plug it in anywhere.

1

It runs from any USB-C source: a laptop, a phone charger or a power bank. There is no battery inside, so it needs a cable to stay on.

2

After 30 seconds without a touch it falls asleep and the screen goes dark. A tap wakes it.

3

Unplugging is fine. It remembers its hearts and how long it has been, so it may be a little hungry when you come back.

Set up WiFi

  1. Double tap, go to WiFi, swipe right. Or hold the BOOT button on the back for 2 seconds.
  2. On your phone, join the network called CITIZEN followed by four letters.
  3. A page opens. Pick your WiFi, type the password, save.

Chat

Hold the screen for a second and the QR appears. Anyone can scan it with a phone camera to chat with your citizen. Tap to close.

No WiFi? The pet still works. Replies wait and arrive next time it connects.

Tap moves to the next item. Swipe right changes it, swipe left changes it back. Hold, or wait 20 seconds, to leave.

The badge checks for a new version when it syncs on WiFi. When there is one, Update shows "NEW" and the version number. The badge installs it by itself when you have not touched it for 10 seconds on the home screen (or swipe right on Update to do it yourself); it downloads it, restarts, and keeps hearts and settings. This version does not check a signature on the download, so only use the official server.

When something looks wrong

Quick fixes.

The screen is off

It is asleep. Tap it. If nothing happens, check the USB-C cable is plugged into something with power.

The colours look odd

Tell us. It is a setting we can fix with an update.

No reply on the badge

It needs WiFi to fetch messages. Set it up from Settings → WiFi, then wait a minute.

Something else is off

Try an update: Settings → Update. If that does not help, message the PROMPT:KL team.
